## Snake Game 🐍👑

[▶ Play Snake Game](https://catherineespejo.github.io/)

Guide the blue crowned snake to the pink food and build your score.

### How to Play

- Use the arrow keys on a keyboard to guide the snake.
- On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen direction buttons.
- Eat the pink food to grow and increase your score.
- Avoid running into the walls or the snake's own body.

### Features

- Blue snake with a crown
- Pink food
- Score and best-score tracking
- Keyboard and touchscreen controls
